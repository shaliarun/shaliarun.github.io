01 / Problem

PROBLEM

Core Challenge

Traditional sports e-commerce filters create heavy decision fatigue, forcing users through exhausting specifications to find specialized gear. SportZone AI replaces complex grids with a conversational assistant, reducing discovery friction to deliver the perfect match in under three clicks.

Takeaway

THE REAL INSIGHT

Designing with SportZone's clean light-teal theme highlights the absolute importance of visual continuity. A conversational assistant is only as effective as the visual trust it builds. Ensuring a clean, uncluttered dual-panel canvas directly reinforces shopper confidence during high-energy discovery steps.
